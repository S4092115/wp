How does on boil rice (NOT STOLEN)

  1. Measure 1 cup of rice and rinse it under cold water.
  2. In a pot, add the rinsed rice and 2 cups of water.
  3. Bring the water to a boil over medium-high heat.
  4. Once boiling, reduce the heat to low and cover the pot with a lid.
  5. Simmer for 18-20 minutes or until all the water is absorbed.
  6. Remove the pot from heat and let it sit covered for 5 minutes.
  7. Fluff the rice with a fork and serve.
